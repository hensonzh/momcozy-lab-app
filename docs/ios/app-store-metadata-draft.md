## Simplified Chinese draft

### Subtitle

```text
AI 母婴照护与专业支持
```

### Promotional text

```text
用 AI 陪伴连接妈妈与宝宝的日常记录、提醒和专业支持，让重要信息更清晰、更容易持续。
```

### Description

```text
momcozy AI 为妈妈和宝宝提供一体化的日常照护支持。

你可以使用 Cozymate AI 整理问题和记录，管理妈妈与宝宝的日常信息，查看日程与提醒，并在可用时连接专业服务。

主要功能：
• AI 陪伴：围绕母婴照护问题进行对话，并结合你主动提供的信息提供整理和建议。
• 妈妈记录：管理哺乳、泌乳、休息、情绪和其他日常照护记录。
• 宝宝记录：管理喂养、尿布、睡眠、成长等宝宝照护信息。
• 日程与提醒：集中查看个人任务、预约和服务计划。
• 专业支持：在服务可用时完成预约准备并参加一对一视频咨询。
• 安全控制：可管理通知、隐私授权和账号删除。

momcozy AI 不能替代医生的诊断或紧急医疗服务。如遇紧急情况或对健康状况有疑问，请及时联系当地医疗机构或合格专业人士。

部分功能需要登录、网络连接或单独购买符合条件的专业服务。实际功能可能因地区和服务可用性而异。
```

## English (U.S.) draft

### Subtitle

```text
AI care for moms and babies
```

### Promotional text

```text
Bring maternal and baby care records, reminders, AI support, and available professional services together in one place.
```

### Description

```text
momcozy AI brings everyday support for moms and babies into one connected experience.

Use Cozymate AI to organize questions and information you choose to share, keep maternal and baby care records, review schedules and reminders, and access available professional services.

Key features:
• AI support for organizing maternal and baby care questions and information.
• Maternal records for feeding, lactation, rest, mood, and other care details.
• Baby records for feeding, diapers, sleep, growth, and everyday care.
• Schedules and reminders for personal tasks, appointments, and care plans.
• Appointment preparation and one-to-one video consultation when available.
• Controls for notifications, privacy authorizations, and account deletion.

momcozy AI does not replace medical diagnosis or emergency services. Contact a qualified healthcare professional or local emergency service when appropriate.

Some features require an account, internet access, or a separate purchase of an eligible professional service. Availability may vary by region.
```

## Screenshot plan

Use synthetic accounts and data only. Do not show real maternal, baby, health, payment, or consultation information.

Recommended story:

1. Welcome/login and privacy entry points.
2. Cozymate AI home or a safe synthetic conversation.
3. Mom overview and one representative record flow.
4. Baby overview and growth/feeding record flow.
5. Schedule and reminder overview.
6. Professional support/consultation preparation, only if enabled for launch.

Current target supports iPhone and iPad. Prepare both device classes unless the release is intentionally changed to iPhone only. Capture final screenshots only after the brand icon, production configuration, and Golden review are complete.
